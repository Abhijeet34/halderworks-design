Text field

rework
Spoken form, lower case
Expected 64 hex characters. That value is 6.
For
Takes a short typed value.
Not for
Long text (textarea); picking from a known list (select).
Anatomy
A 13px/560 label above at 4px; the box at least --hw-control-h with --hw-field-pad-y and --hw-field-pad-x inside, a 1px --hw-line-strong edge (3:1), radius --hw-radius-md; a hint or an error sentence below at 12px.
States
rest, hover (edge to muted), focus-visible (edge and inset ring), invalid (danger edge plus an error sentence with an icon), disabled, read-only (dashed edge on --hw-bg-subtle)
Keyboard
Native input; the label is a real label, and the error is tied with aria-describedby.
Do
Say what is wrong and what is expected in the error.
Do not
Use the placeholder as the label. Turn the edge red without a sentence.

Hotkey field

new
Dictate
Open History
Paste lastmacOS uses ⌘V for Paste. Choose another.
For
Shows the shortcut that starts something, and records a new one when pressed.
Not for
Typing text (a text field); naming a key in prose (a keycap).
Anatomy
The text field box as a button, holding the current shortcut as keycaps. While recording it is pressed, with a focus-coloured inset edge and a muted prompt; a clash shows the field error below it.
States
set, recording (aria-pressed), invalid (a clash, with a sentence), disabled
Keyboard
A native button: Enter or Space starts recording, the next chord is taken, Escape cancels. Capturing the chord is the product's script; this layer draws the states.
Do
Show the shortcut in the platform's symbols, as the menu bar will.
Do not
Accept a chord the system already owns without saying so.
Tokens
--hw-border-w, --hw-control-h, --hw-danger, --hw-field-pad-x, --hw-field-pad-y, --hw-fill, --hw-focus, --hw-font-sans, --hw-line-strong, --hw-radius-md, --hw-space-4, --hw-surface, --hw-text, --hw-text-disabled, --hw-text-muted, --hw-text-sm

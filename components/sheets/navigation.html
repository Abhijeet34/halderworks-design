For
Switches between views of the same thing.
Not for
Moving between places (rail); filtering (segmented control).
Anatomy
13px labels 16px apart on a 1px line, each at control height; the active tab has a 2px rule in --hw-text and weight 620; a count may follow in muted. Tabs wrap at large text sizes.
States
rest, hover, selected, focus-visible
Keyboard
The layer styles role tab; the product owns the panels and the arrow-key roving between tabs, since the panels are its own.

Keep to three to six.
Do not

--hw-border-w, --hw-control-h, --hw-font-sans, --hw-line, --hw-space-16, --hw-tab-active-w, --hw-text, --hw-text-muted, --hw-text-sm

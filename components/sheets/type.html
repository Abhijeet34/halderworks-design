Microphone

Cited 1,204 times, against 87.

For
Sets text on the house type ramp by its job, on any element.
Not for
Choosing a heading level: the element says the level, the class says the size.
Anatomy
Headline 3xl/700 in the display face, title 2xl/650, heading xl/650, subheading lg/600, lead lg in muted, body md, small sm in muted, label xs/550 in muted; lead and body stop at --hw-measure-prose. hw-num sets tabular figures on any of them. Each zeroes its margins.
States
static; every size follows html[data-text-size]
Keyboard
Not focusable; a heading class on a real h1 to h6 keeps the outline a screen reader walks.
Do
Put the class on the element whose level is right, and one headline per page.
Do not
Use a heading class on a div to fake a heading. Set a size in px beside it.
Tokens
--hw-font-display, --hw-font-display-stretch, --hw-font-sans, --hw-leading, --hw-leading-snug, --hw-leading-tight, --hw-measure-prose, --hw-text-2xl, --hw-text-3xl, --hw-text-lg, --hw-text-md, --hw-text-muted, --hw-text-sm, --hw-text-xl, --hw-text-xs

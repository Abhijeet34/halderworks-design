Menu

Sort by

For
Lists actions on a thing, opened from a button or a secondary click.
Not for
Picking a value (select); navigation (rail).
Anatomy
A raised surface with a 4px inset; items at control height minus 2px with a 1.15em icon and the shortcut right-aligned in muted; a 1px separator; the destructive item last, in danger.
States
item rest, active (fill), checked (tick), disabled, destructive
Keyboard
Opened as a popover from its button, so Escape and an outside click close it and focus returns. Items are buttons in a column: Tab moves through them. Arrow-key roving is the product's.
Do
Put the destructive item last, after a separator.
Do not
Nest more than one level.
Tokens
--hw-border-w, --hw-control-h, --hw-fill-hover, --hw-line, --hw-radius-lg, --hw-radius-sm, --hw-shadow-overlay, --hw-space-16, --hw-space-4, --hw-space-8, --hw-surface-raised, --hw-text, --hw-text-disabled, --hw-text-muted, --hw-text-sm, --hw-text-xs, --hw-z-dropdown

Tooltip

Copy ⌘CCopy ⌘CThe second is drawn open, as it shows after the delay.
For
Names an icon-only control and its shortcut.
Not for
Anything the person needs in order to act: it is unreachable on touch.
Anatomy
An ink box, 12px/500, at most 18rem wide, radius --hw-radius-sm, 8px from its control. Wrap the control in .hw-tip; it shows above, or below with .hw-tip--below.
States
hidden; shown 500ms after hover or keyboard focus of the control, hidden at once; dismissed by Escape until the pointer or focus leaves
Keyboard
Shown on focus as well as hover, from CSS alone; Escape hides it without moving the pointer or focus (components/radiogroup.js). The control keeps its own aria-label; the tip repeats it for sighted readers.
Do
Show it on focus as well as hover.
Do not
Put a sentence of help in it.
Tokens
--hw-duration-fast, --hw-ease-out, --hw-font-sans, --hw-ink, --hw-leading-snug, --hw-on-ink, --hw-radius-sm, --hw-space-8, --hw-text-xs, --hw-z-dropdown

Shows a small panel of detail or controls tied to one thing on screen.
Not for
A task with its own flow (dialog).
Anatomy
A raised surface, 16px padding, 20rem wide, --hw-shadow-overlay; opens below its anchor and flips above or across at the edge, where the engine supports anchor positioning.
States
closed, open

For
Asks for a decision that blocks the task until it is answered.
Not for
Information (callout); a confirmation that fits its row (inline confirm).
Anatomy
A raised surface at radius --hw-radius-lg, 24px padding, a 21px title, the body, actions right with the primary last; the scrim behind.
States
open (320ms rise, none under reduced motion), closing
Keyboard
A native dialog opened with showModal or a command button: focus moves in, is held there, and returns on close; Escape closes it.

'Are you sure?' with OK and Cancel.

Inline confirm

Remove Mira Shah from this project?
For
Confirms a destructive action on one row without leaving it.
Not for
Bulk or irreversible deletes of many things (dialog).
Anatomy
Replaces the row's actions at the row's height: a sentence, the danger button, Cancel. It wraps rather than overflows at large text sizes.
States
open; it never times out
Keyboard
Focus moves to Cancel when it opens and returns to the row's action when it closes.
Do
Repeat the verb on the button.
Do not
Time it out.
Tokens
--hw-border-w, --hw-line, --hw-row-h, --hw-space-12, --hw-space-8, --hw-text, --hw-text-sm

Markup

<!-- In place of the row's actions -->
<div class="hw-confirm">
  <span>Remove <b>Mira Shah</b> from this project?</span>
  <div class="hw-confirm-actions">
    <button type="button" class="hw-btn hw-btn--sm hw-btn--danger">Remove</button>
    <button type="button" class="hw-btn hw-btn--sm hw-btn--quiet">Cancel</button>
  </div>
</div>

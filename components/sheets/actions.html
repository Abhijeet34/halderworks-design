Button

rework
For
Starts an action whose verb is its label.
Not for
Moving to another page (a link); choosing between options (segmented control).
Anatomy
A box at least --hw-control-h tall in three sizes, 12px side padding at M, radius --hw-radius-md, a 13px label at weight 560, an optional icon at 1.15em with an 8px gap. Variants: secondary (the default), primary in ink, accent, quiet, danger outlined and danger solid, and icon-only.
States
rest, hover (fill), active (1px press), focus-visible (2px ring at 2px offset), disabled (fill, disabled ink, no border), busy (a spinner before the label; still under reduced motion)
Keyboard
A native button: Tab reaches it, Enter and Space press it. An icon-only button carries aria-label.
Do
One primary per view, in ink. Keep the accent variant for a stage or the one brand moment a product names.
Do not
Two primaries side by side. Colour a button to make it louder. Disable a submit because a form is incomplete.
Tokens
--hw-border-w, --hw-control-h, --hw-duration-fast, --hw-ease-out, --hw-fill, --hw-font-sans, --hw-line-strong, --hw-radius-md, --hw-space-12, --hw-space-8, --hw-surface, --hw-text, --hw-text-disabled, --hw-text-sm

Link

keep

The report cites doi:10.1038/s41586-019-1666-5, which resolves to a different title.

For
Moves the reader somewhere else; underlined, so it never relies on colour.
Not for
Starting an action in place (a button).
Anatomy
Inline text in --hw-accent-text with a 1px underline at 0.18em offset, 2px under the pointer. In print an external link prints its address after it.
States
rest, hover, focus-visible; visited is left unstyled because tools are revisited
Keyboard
A native anchor: Tab reaches it, Enter follows it.
Do
Write the destination as the link text.
Do not
Write 'click here'. Remove the underline in running text.
Tokens
--hw-accent-text, --hw-text-muted, --hw-text-xs

Keycap

new

Hold ⌥ Space in any app and speak. Let go to type it there. Open History with ⌘2.

For
Names a key or a shortcut the person presses.
Not for
Code (the code mark); a pressable control (a button).
Anatomy
Mono at 0.8em and never under 11px, a 1px --hw-line-strong border with a 2px bottom edge, radius --hw-radius-sm, on --hw-surface.
States
static
Keyboard
Not focusable: it names a key, it is not one.
Do
Use the platform's symbols: ⌘ ⌥ ⇧ ⌃.
Do not
Spell out 'Cmd'. Use it as a pressable control.
Tokens
--hw-border-w, --hw-font-mono, --hw-line-strong, --hw-radius-sm, --hw-surface, --hw-text

Segmented control

keep anatomy, rem sizing
For
Chooses one of two to five mutually exclusive views or values in place.
Not for
Moving between pages (tabs, rail); more than five options (select).
Anatomy
A track on --hw-fill with a 2px inset; the selected segment sits on --hw-surface with a 1px --hw-line-strong edge. Segments wrap rather than overflow at large text sizes.
States
rest, hover, selected, focus-visible, disabled
Keyboard
A radio group: one Tab stop on the selected segment, arrow keys move the choice, Home and End jump to the ends, disabled segments are skipped. components/radiogroup.js is the whole script.
Do
Label every segment with a word.
Do not
Use icons alone. Animate the selection sliding.
Tokens
--hw-border-w, --hw-control-h, --hw-fill, --hw-focus, --hw-focus-offset, --hw-focus-w, --hw-font-sans, --hw-line-strong, --hw-radius-md, --hw-radius-sm, --hw-space-12, --hw-surface, --hw-text, --hw-text-disabled, --hw-text-muted, --hw-text-sm
